04  /  Engagement Framework

From email
to working system.

Every new client engagement follows the same six-step sequence. No surprises, no scope creep masquerading as discovery, no hourly billing.

01

Email + discovery call

You email info@adorbistech.com with a short description. Within 24 hours we reply. If there's mutual fit, a 30-minute call gets scheduled.

02

Mutual NDA

Before discussing your systems or data in detail, we execute a mutual NDA. Your standard template or ours.

03

Reference calls

On request, we arrange 1:1 calls with 2–3 current clients in your industry. You ask them anything.

04

Scoped proposal

Written proposal with fixed scope, fixed price, fixed timeline, defined success criteria. No hourly billing.

05

Paid pilot or full engagement

Larger scopes start with a 2–4 week paid pilot. Smaller scopes go direct to full engagement.

06

Delivery, handover, NDA continues

Full source code, infrastructure-as-code, runbooks, and documentation. NDA survives the engagement.
